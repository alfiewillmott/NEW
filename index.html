FAQs

Common questions

Can you really build it in seven days?

Yes. Once your content and brand assets are with us, design and build run in parallel across a fixed seven-day sprint with two review points.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief, your logo files if you have them, and any copy or imagery you want used. If copy is missing, Premium includes writing it for you.

Is the price really fixed?

The package price is what you pay. Scope is agreed up front, and anything beyond it is quoted separately before work begins.

Can I edit the site myself?

Every build ships on a friendly editor so you can update text, images and pages without touching code.

What happens after launch?

Essential includes a two-week snagging window. Premium adds two months of support for tweaks, fixes and small additions.
